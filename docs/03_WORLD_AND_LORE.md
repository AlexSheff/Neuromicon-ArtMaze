## 1. The Premise of the World

**Neuromicon ArtMaze** is an ancient-yet-timeless architectural manifold containing **1149 potential chambers**. It was not built to imprison visitors, nor to congratulate them for solving riddles. It was constructed as an **instrument of perception**.

In the ordinary world, architecture obeys utility: corridors connect destinations, mirrors reflect light faithfully, and maps correspond to territory. Inside ArtMaze, architecture responds to **attention, belief, memory, and choice**.

---

## 2. Core Lore Tenets

### 2.1 The Constant `1149`
`1149` is the curvature constant of the labyrinth. Not all 1149 rooms are built at once; some exist as completed stone sanctuaries, others as half-formed hypotheses, and others as open thresholds into the Void. An unbuilt room is not a missing file—it is an uncollapsed region of the manifold.

### 2.2 Why Objects Cannot Leave Their Room
Physical objects (`plinths`, `monoliths`, `chairs`, `sundials`, `steles`) belong to the local reality specification of their chamber (`portable: false`). A visitor carries nothing in their hands between rooms. The only things capable of crossing a threshold are:
- **Observations & Discoveries** (`discoveries`)
- **Identity Propositions** (`identity`)
- **Decisions & Covenants** (`decisions`)
- **Memory of Prior Visits** (`roomStates`, `cycleCount`)

### 2.3 The Three Orders of Mirrors
1. **The Entry Mirror (`ROOM_0000`)**: Asks what the visitor projects onto the unknown (*"Whom do you expect to meet here?"*).
2. **The Identity Mirror (`ROOM_0107`)**: Proposes an archetype (`THE_ARCHIVIST`, `THE_OBSERVER`, `THE_CREATOR`, `THE_EXPLORER`) that must subsequently be proven through action.
3. **The Meta-Mirror (`ROOM_0999`)**: Strips away archetypes and reflects the visitor's raw history of decisions (*"Was this truly a labyrinth?"*).

### 2.4 The Seven Voids
When a door opens into an unbuilt or infinite region, the visitor steps into one of seven primary Voids:
- `infinity` — Endless receding travertine colonnades.
- `fractal` — Self-similar recursive geometric manifolds.
- `starfield` — Silent astral parallax beyond the stone vaults.
- `mirror` — An unbroken obsidian horizon of pure reflection.
- `fog` — Liminal atmospheric suspension where metric distance dissolves.
- `darkness` — Acoustic stillness beyond visual reference.
- `unknown` — The 1149th boundary where new rooms are born.
