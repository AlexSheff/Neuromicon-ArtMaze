# 01. Cognitive Specification — Neuromicon ArtMaze

**Document ID:** `DOC-01`  
**Layer:** Cognitive Architecture & Experimental Target  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

Neuromicon ArtMaze is designed not as a linear puzzle campaign, but as a **participant transformation protocol**. The visible layer is an architectural labyrinth of rooms, paintings, mirrors, and doors; the structural layer is an environment that surfaces, measures, and cultivates specific modes of thought.

Rather than testing memorized knowledge or twitch reflexes, the world creates situations where habitual templates fail and autonomous model-building becomes necessary.

---

## 2. Design Target — Cognitive Properties

The protocol is calibrated around nine primary cognitive properties:

| Property ID | Dimension (EN) | Измерение (RU) | Behavioral Expression in ArtMaze |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AUTONOMY` | Autonomy | Самостоятельность | Acting without waiting for explicit instructions; declining false binary prompts. |
| `CURIOSITY` | Epistemic Curiosity | Любопытство | Inspecting unannounced objects, inscriptions, shadows, and peripheral rooms without promised reward. |
| `ALT_VISION` | Alternative Vision | Способность видеть альтернативы | Discovering the 4th option (Rabbit Hole) when presented with doors A / B / C. |
| `UNCERTAINTY` | Uncertainty Tolerance | Терпимость к неопределённости | Exploring `void` and `planned` spaces (`fractal`, `infinity`, `starfield`) calmly without immediate retreat. |
| `SYSTEMS` | Systems Thinking | Системное мышление | Recognizing that room states, visit counts, and identity choices form a connected causal graph. |
| `COLLABORATION` | Asymmetric Collaboration | Способность сотрудничать | Synthesizing partial perspectives (`Space`, `Sound`, `Rule`, `Mechanism`) and choosing non-zero-sum trust outcomes. |
| `PERSPECTIVE` | Perspective Revision | Изменение точки зрения | Revising strategy when a room invalidates a previously successful rule (`model_revision`). |
| `GENERATIVE` | Generative Creation | Создание собственных решений | Transitioning from `Player` to `Designer` by authoring rules, inscriptions, or rooms (`ROOM_SPEC`). |
| `RESPONSIBILITY` | Choice Responsibility | Ответственность за выбор | Proving a declared Mirror identity through subsequent room actions (`Identity → Action → Consequence`). |

## 3. Baseline vs. Transformation

The system distinguishes between:

1. **Baseline Vector (Stages I–III)**:
   - How the participant behaves immediately upon entering `ROOM_0000`:
     - Safe vs. risky door selection;
     - Decision latency;
     - Adherence to explicit instructions vs. unprompted environmental inspection;
     - Response to the First Mirror (`"Whom do you expect to meet here?"`).
2. **Tension & Revision Vector (Stages IV–X)**:
   - How the participant responds when logic, geometry, reflection, or memory contradicts expectations.
3. **Agency & Meta-Awareness Vector (Stages XI–XVI)**:
   - Whether the participant modifies the system (`Creator Room`), reflects on their own trajectory in the `Meta-Mirror`, and intentionally experiments with a new strategy on a second pass (`Cycle 2+`).

---

## 4. Non-Diagnostic Principle

ArtMaze never presents clinical or psychological diagnoses, IQ scores, or static personality boxes. All observations describe **how the participant interacts with an ambiguous reality**.
