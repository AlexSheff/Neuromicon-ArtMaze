## 4. Audio Generation Audit

- **Mechanism:** `src/systems/audio/spatialAudioSystem.ts` synthesizes three oscillator banks (`ascentOscs` at `220/329.63/440 Hz`, `descentOscs` at `73.42/110/146.83 Hz`, `roomOscs` at `108/162/216 Hz`).
- **Gaps Found:**
  1. `AudioContext` was not unlocked by a dedicated Entry button gesture before entering the world (§2.1.2 & §8).
  2. In the Threshold Hall, vertical audio crossfade was static (`0.5`) instead of dynamically responding to **head pitch** (`looking up` brings `ascent-layer`, `looking down` brings `descent-layer` per §2.2 Beat 45–70s & §8).
  3. Missing spatial interaction cues (pedestal resonance hum, reveal chord, 3-second platform hold pulse, and toggleable audio captions per §2.8 & §8).

## 5. README Claims vs. Code Discrepancies

1. **`R` Key Collision:** `README.md` listed `Q / R` for Snap Turn AND `R` for Rules Overlay, causing an active keybinding collision.
2. **SHA-256 Verification & 8s Timeout:** `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` stated that room modules are verified via SHA-256 `entryHash` and fall back on failure, but `roomStreamer.ts` mounted static objects without running `crypto.subtle.digest('SHA-256', ...)` or enforcing the **8-second Void fallback timeout** (§7.2.3).
3. **Quest 2 Foveation & Dynamic Resolution Scaler:** Not yet enabled on `renderer.xr` (§6).
